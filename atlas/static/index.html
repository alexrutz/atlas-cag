      Each document is prefilled into llama.cpp once and its KV cache is persisted to disk. A question restores that cache into a slot and only evaluates the conversation and your question.

      1. Choose a modelSettings: create a llama-server preset or download a model.
      2. Add documentsLibrary: upload files into collections; PDF tools cut large PDFs into shards first.
      3. ChatTick documents or whole collections on the left and ask. Follow-up questions keep the conversation.
