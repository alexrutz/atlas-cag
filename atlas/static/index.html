    Ask your documents

    Each document is prefilled into llama.cpp once and its KV cache is persisted to disk. A query restores that cache into a slot and only evaluates your question.

    1. Choose a modelOpen Settings to create a llama-server preset or download a model.
    2. IngestUpload files into collections. Atlas prefills each one with zero output tokens and saves the slot.
    3. AskTick documents or whole collections. One document answers directly; several are answered one by one and then combined.
